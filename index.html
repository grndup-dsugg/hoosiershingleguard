Know before you commit

Is your roof a good fit?

Not every roof is a good candidate for treatment. It starts with the condition of your shingles.

May be a good fit
  • Asphalt shingles that remain intact and securely attached
  • A roof that's aging but has no major damage
  • An otherwise sound roof you want to keep longer
May not be a good fit
  • Missing, curling, or severely cracked shingles
  • Active leaks or damaged roof decking
  • Severe granule loss or other extensive deterioration

These are general guidelines, not a diagnosis. Eligibility, treatment compatibility, and any warranty considerations should be confirmed before application. This service does not repair leaks or structural damage.
